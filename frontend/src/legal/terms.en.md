# Terms of use

Version 1.0, 26 October 2026. These are the main rules for using TibaDirect. TibaDirect is a prototype built for a hackathon: it runs on PayPal sandbox money with made-up hospitals and bills, and nothing on it is a real fundraiser. These terms describe how the service is meant to work when it is live, and have not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

## What TibaDirect does

TibaDirect helps families raise money for a hospital bill and helps donors, including people abroad, give with confidence. Donations are paid to the confirmed hospital and never to the family.

## We do not keep donations

TibaDirect does not hold money for itself and does not pay families. In production, donations are collected by an approved partner or a registered non-profit that PayPal has cleared to take donations, and paid to the hospital. Every movement is written in a public ledger whose entries are chained together so changes can be detected.

## How the hospital is paid

- A page goes live only after the hospital's billing office confirms the bill with a one-time code sent to its official address. Bills above the hospital's limit need a second approval by a different person.
- The hospital is paid in parts as money arrives, never in advance of a donation. While a bill is an estimate, no more than 80% of it is paid until the final bill is confirmed.
- Donors see each payment on the case page.

## Leftover money: your choice when you give

Sometimes more is given than the final bill needs, or treatment ends early. When you donate you choose what happens to your share of what is left:

- **Refund me:** your share is refunded to the PayPal account you paid with. Refunds under US$1 cost more than they return, so a leftover that small goes to the solidarity pool instead.
- **Solidarity pool:** your share moves to a pool that helps other confirmed cases.
- **Follow-up care:** your share is held for 14 days in case the same patient needs more care, then refunded.

Money is never sent anywhere you did not choose. PayPal's fee on a donation is not refunded.

## When a case ends

- If the family cancels (treatment stops or the patient dies), nothing more is paid out and each donor's leftover follows their choice.
- If we freeze a case because it looks fraudulent, the page is paused and every donor is refunded in full, whatever they chose. Payments already sent to the hospital cannot be recalled.
- A closed case stays on the site as a public record.

## Families

You must upload your own genuine bill and must not give false information. Do not upload anything you are not allowed to share. A false case is frozen, closed and may be reported.

## Hospitals

Billing staff confirm only bills their hospital really issued, using the code sent to the hospital's official address. Do not share your sign-in or a code.

## Donors

Give only with money that is yours. A donation is final once the bill it helps is paid, except where these terms say it is refunded.

## Software decisions

Software only flags. People decide: the hospital confirms every bill, and our staff review anything flagged. Software never rejects a case.

## No guarantees

We verify that a bill is real, not that a story is complete or that treatment will succeed. The service is provided as it is, and we are not responsible for the hospital's care.
