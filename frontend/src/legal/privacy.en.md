# Privacy notice

Version 1.0, 26 October 2026. This notice explains what personal data TibaDirect handles, why, for how long, and what you can do about it. TibaDirect is a prototype built for a hackathon and runs on PayPal sandbox money; it follows the rules of the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 as if it were live. It has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer or by the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

## Who is responsible

The TibaDirect team decides why and how your data is used (the "data controller"). Until a legal entity or a licensed partner runs the service, questions and requests go to the address under "Contact" below.

## What we collect and why

- **Families:** an email address (to sign you in and tell you what is happening), the name you choose for the page (an alias, not a real name), your county or town, the hospital, the patient or admission number from the bill, whether the patient is a child, an optional story, and the bill itself.
- **Hospital billing staff:** an email address and a role, to sign you in and to record who confirmed a bill.
- **Donors:** no account. We keep the amount, the time, the PayPal payment reference, your choice for leftover money, and a name only if you type one. PayPal handles your payment details; we never see or store a card number or PayPal password.
- **Visitors:** one cookie that keeps you signed in or protects a form. We run no advertising and no tracking, and we do not use analytics that follow you.

## Health data and children

A hospital bill is health information. We reduce it as early as we can: names, phone numbers, ID numbers, the patient number and medical terms (diagnoses, procedures, medicines) are removed from the bill's text before it is stored or looked at by software. Pages never show a diagnosis. A parent or legal guardian must give consent for a child.

## Your consents

Each use has its own consent, written in plain words next to the box you tick: letting the hospital confirm the bill, showing a public page, analysis by a service outside Kenya, and updates to donors. You can withdraw a consent at any time from your dashboard. Withdrawing the public page hides it at once. Withdrawing the hospital check before the hospital confirms cancels the case.

## Automated checks, and people

Software reads the bill, looks for signs that it was changed or used before, and gives a risk band. It never approves or rejects a case. A person reviews anything flagged, the hospital confirms every bill, and you can ask a person to look at your case at any time.

## Analysis outside Kenya

Only if you agree, a language model service outside Kenya may read the redacted text of your bill (never the file, names, phone numbers or the patient number). Without your consent, only our own servers are used and a person may need to look at your case.

## Who else sees data

- The **hospital** named on the case: the bill, the patient number you entered, and the amounts.
- **PayPal**: to take donations, refund them and pay the hospital.
- **Our hosting and email providers**, who process data for us.
- **Public pages** show the alias, county, hospital, amounts and your story. A donor's name shows in the public ledger only if the donor typed one and did not ask to be anonymous.
- We do not sell data and we do not share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

- **The bill file:** deleted 24 hours after the hospital confirms it, or 24 hours after a case is cancelled. A record of the checks (without personal details) stays.
- **Sign-in codes:** ten minutes. **Sessions:** seven days.
- **Money records and the public ledger:** kept, because they are the proof that donations were handled correctly. They hold no email addresses.
- **Cases and consents:** kept as the record of what you agreed to and when.
- **Appeal checker:** what you paste is turned into a fingerprint and the text is not kept.
- **Logs:** carry no names, email addresses, phone numbers or patient numbers.

## Keeping it safe

Data is encrypted in transit and the patient number is encrypted at rest. Access is limited by role, hospital staff must use a one-time code sent to the hospital's official address to confirm a bill, and every sensitive action is recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited. If a breach puts you at risk we will tell you and the ODPC within the time the law sets.

## Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, have it deleted, object to its use, or receive a copy. Some money records must stay for accounting, but they carry no contact details. Write to us and we will answer as fast as we can, and within the time the law sets.

## Complaints

You may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya (www.odpc.go.ke) at any time.
