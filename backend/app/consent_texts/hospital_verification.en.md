# Let the hospital confirm the bill

I agree that TibaDirect may send the hospital named on this case the bill I upload, the patient reference I enter, and the amount and dates on the bill. TibaDirect will ask the hospital's official billing contact, listed in the public facility register, to confirm that the bill is real.

The hospital uses this only to confirm the bill and to receive payments for it. TibaDirect does not collect or store any diagnosis or test results.

I can withdraw this before the hospital confirms the bill. If I do, the case is cancelled.
