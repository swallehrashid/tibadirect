# Allow automated checks outside Kenya

I agree that a redacted copy of my bill's text may be checked by an automated service that runs outside Kenya. Before anything is sent, names, phone numbers, ID numbers and the patient reference are removed.

If I do not agree, TibaDirect checks the bill only on its own servers, and a person may need to look at the case, which can take longer. The checks never approve or reject a case: the hospital and TibaDirect staff decide.

I can withdraw this at any time. Bills I upload afterwards are then checked only on TibaDirect's servers.
